MONOS

Learn about the Islamic faith and culture with a user-friendly, engaging app: a comprehensive platform for exploring Islamic teachings, culture, and community.

01Discover

The problem

Problem statement

Many people struggle to learn about Islamic faith and culture due to fragmented resources and lack of structured guidance. There's a need for a platform with structured learning paths, interactive content, and accessible support for exploring Islamic teachings, culture, and community.

02Research

The landscape

Background research

I analysed existing Islamic learning platforms and resources, examining their features, teaching methods, and user experiences, to understand learners' needs and identify gaps in current resources.

03Define

Findings

Findings

Users need structured learning paths, interactive content, and accessible information. Many existing resources lack personalisation and fail to provide comprehensive information. A user-friendly platform could make learning about Islamic culture engaging and accessible.

04Develop

Wireframes

Lo-fi wireframes

Low-fidelity wireframes defined the layout of key screens, the navigation structure, and how content is organised.

05Deliver

High fidelity

Hi-fi design

The high-fidelity screens were created after user testing the lo-fi wireframes. Users loved the clear navigation and the easy-to-use interface, which made the improved app more visually appealing and user-friendly.

(06) Reflect / Key learnings

A comprehensive Islamic hub that makes exploring Islamic teachings engaging and user-friendly.

Through an iterative design process, I developed MONOS to help users learn about Islamic faith and culture. The app features structured learning paths, interactive content, and accessible information.
